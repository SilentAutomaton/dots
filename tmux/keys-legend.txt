HOW TO READ THE KEYS
  C-x      Ctrl and x held together          C-Space  the prefix: Ctrl+Space
  M-x      Alt and x held together           M-Left   Alt and the left arrow
  C-M-x    Ctrl, Alt and x held together     Space    the space bar
  Shift    a capital letter is Shift and the letter; Alt+Shift switches the keyboard layout, so no Alt bindings use it
  Alt-bindings need no prefix. Letters also work on the Russian layout, on the same physical key.
